The URL is the contract — schema v1

Every input lives in the query string, in this order, and nothing else does. A sealed plan is SHA-256 of the canonical string of the plan keys alone (everything after ? up to and excluding looks), so the same plan always produces the same receipt whatever was observed later.

Numbers are written in their shortest exact decimal form. Method: gsDesign — test.type 4 (one-tailed: binding efficacy bound, non-binding futility bound) or 2 (two-tailed symmetric), Kim-DeMets power spending α·t³ for efficacy and β·t² for futility, sample size from power.prop.test; the z-statistic is testBinomial (Miettinen–Nurminen), the sequential p-value gsProbability, the interval ciBinomial at the look's efficacy bound. The same calls, in the same order, as Merritt Aho's sequential-test-app on forwarddigital.org/tools.
