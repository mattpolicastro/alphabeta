The URL is the contract — schema v1

One relation, two unknowns: fix the lift and it gives you the n, fix the n and it gives you the lift. solve says which. Every input lives in the query string, in this order, and nothing else does. A sealed result is SHA-256 of the canonical string (everything after ?), so the same inputs always produce the same receipt — and a receipt is a receipt for one question, because solve is inside the hash.

Numbers are written in their shortest exact decimal form (0.1, not 0.10); the parser accepts either. Visitors per variant under solve=mde is ⌈traffic × days / variants⌉. Method for both directions: two-proportion z-test, equal arms, no continuity correction — R's power.prop.test, the same convention as the reference calculator on forwarddigital.org/tools. solve=mde runs it backwards, finding the smallest positive relative lift that reaches the stated power at that per-arm n by bisection to 1e-12.

The solve for toggle flips the unknown in place and carries the inputs across: baseline, variants, tails, alpha and power verbatim, the computed runtime into days, and the computed lift into mde (mdeKind=relative, rounded up at 6 dp so the trip back never asks for more visitors than you already had). /lab/detectable-lift/ is kept as a redirect: it maps its old schema onto solve=mde and forwards here.
