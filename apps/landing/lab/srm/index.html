Is my split broken?

Checks whether the split you observed between variants is consistent with the split you configured. A mismatch means the assignment itself is off — bot filtering, a redirect, a caching layer, a bug — and the test result cannot be trusted until it is found.

Comma-separated, one per arm: weights (50,50), shares (0.5,0.5) or ratios (1,3) — only the proportions matter.

Same order as the allocation. Unique visitors assigned, not sessions.

0.001 is the convention. SRM checks run continuously over a test's life, so a loose α (0.05) false-alarms constantly; 0.001 keeps the alarm meaningful and still catches a broken split fast.

The URL is the contract — schema v1

Every input lives in the query string, in this order, and nothing else does. A sealed result is SHA-256 of the canonical string (everything after ?), so the same inputs always produce the same receipt.

Numbers are written in their shortest exact decimal form (0.1, not 0.10); lists use a literal comma, and the parser also accepts %2C. Method: Pearson's chi-square goodness of fit, χ² = Σ (observed − expected)² / expected with expected = share × total observed, on arms − 1 degrees of freedom, no continuity correction — scipy.stats.chisquare. The p-value is the upper tail, computed from the regularized incomplete gamma function.
