alphaBeta · three brand direction studies

Constraint: structured & professional core, lightness at the edges. Explicitly avoiding warm-paper AI-tool defaults and Memphis confetti. Cross-cutting principle in all three: state-as-style — draft and locked content are typeset differently, so commitment is legible at a glance.

Study 1 · The Registry

Notary meets modern grotesk. Cold near-white, hairline rules, tabular mono for anything that is data. One color exists — sealing-wax red — and it is spent ONLY on commitment: the fold-if, the seal, the margin note. Lightness = the stamp's slight rotation, inked marginalia.
